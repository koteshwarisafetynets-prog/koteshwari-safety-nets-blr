## 5. Photo Best Practices for Team Members
1. **Lighting**: Take photos in daylight (morning 9am - 12pm or afternoon 3pm - 5pm).
2. **Angle**: Include both wide shots showing the whole balcony and detail shots showing the anchor points and clean finish.
3. **Cleanliness**: Ensure the balcony floor is swept and free of debris after drilling before capturing the "After" picture.
4. **Format**: Save as JPG/PNG from smartphone camera; run through `scripts/migrate-images.js` to automatically resize to 1400px and convert to webp.
